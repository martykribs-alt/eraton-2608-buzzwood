The Planning Official approved the Site Design Review for 16 single-unit detached houses sharing a common lot at 602 S 15th Street, map tax lot 12612CD01500, file P24-08, with conditions; date of decision January 10, 2025. [NOD p.1, p.3]

The applicable criteria are Philomath Municipal Code 18.105.040, 18.40.030 and Division 3 Design Standards; the zone is High Density Residential (R-3). [NOD p.1]

The application was complete on December 10, 2024. [NOD p.1]

Approval is based on the criteria in PMC 18.110.060, with the findings of fact and conditions of approval in the Staff Report dated January 8, 2025. [NOD p.1]

The subject property is 1.59 acres. [NOD Findings]

The City Council approved annexation of the property and a rezone to R-3 on December 9, 2024. [NOD Findings]

A Development Agreement was signed limiting the development to 16 single-unit detached properties and building heights not exceeding 30 feet. [NOD Findings]

The lot is in the 1% flood zone, and there are wetlands in the middle portion of the lot. [NOD Findings]

The existing single-family residence and two outbuildings will be razed with this development. [NOD Findings]

A fire lane will connect the houses sharing the common lot to 15th Street; City utilities are available at the property line. [NOD Findings]

If the development is built in more than one phase, each phase must be self-sufficient with respect to utilities and infrastructure. [NOD Condition 1]

Wetland or wetland mitigation permits must be approved by the State of Oregon and/or the Federal Government before any building permit is issued. [NOD Condition 2]

A floodplain permit must be submitted for each structure, and each structure must be built without the use of fill, since FEMA is not allowing any LOMR or CLOMR. [NOD Condition 3]

A no-rise report for the entire site must accompany the building permits; the floodplain permit must be issued before building permit issuance; building permit drawings must show flow-through vents for each structure and comply with PMC 18.55.150(B)(1). [NOD Condition 3]

One dedicated, covered bicycle parking space is required per dwelling unit, and an updated site plan showing bicycle parking must be submitted before building permits are issued. [NOD Condition 4]

With the building permit, the applicant must submit scalable elevation drawings showing eaves projecting more than six inches and window trim wider than four inches. [NOD Condition 5]

A landscape plan must show retained trees from the tree conservation plan, drought-tolerant plants or an irrigation system and its location and type, documentation for drought-resistant natives, and the height of buffer screening plants at the side and rear yards. [NOD Condition 6]

One new street tree of at least 2.5-inch caliper is required, at the location shown on the site plan. [NOD Condition 7]

The property owner must extend a new off-site sidewalk north to a new pedestrian ramp aligned with the existing south ramp at the Timothy Street intersection before any Certificate of Occupancy for Phase I. [NOD Condition 8]

The pedestrian pathway inside the condominium complex must connect to the 15th Street sidewalk and meet ADA width and grade requirements; a walkway from the north fire turnaround is recommended. [NOD Condition 9]

Lighting of the fire lane, parking, bicycle parking and pathways is required, cast downward, and must be shown on a revised site plan. [NOD Condition 10]

An updated site drawing must show the dimensions and location of at least one ADA parking space. [NOD Condition 11]

An updated utility drawing for the revised layout must show a storm drainage and detention system meeting PWDS standards. [NOD Condition 12]

A new fire hydrant is required; the fire district accepts the location proposed next to the driveway entrance. [NOD Condition 13]

The decision may be appealed to the Land Use Board of Appeals within 14 calendar days of the date of decision. [NOD p.3]
