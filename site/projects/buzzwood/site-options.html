How it conforms
  • Every home is 20 ft or more from the wetland, the pond and its neighbors.

  • An SDR minor modification: 9 homes in 2 phases instead of 16 in 3. The DA allows "up to 16."
  • A field check of the south tree row under A1–A3.

Code and entitlement findings behind these plans

Research of 2 Nov 2026: City documents, the recorded DA, SDR P24-08, DSL WD 2025-0069

Entitlement clocks

  • DA §12(a): all Phase 1 permits within 1 year of the final SDR (24 Jan 2026); Phase 2 complete by about 24 Jan 2028; every phase by 24 Jan 2030, or the approval expires. The City Manager can extend each phase up to 12 months for delays outside the owner's control.
  • The owner's reading that the 602 renovation permit (675-25-000155-STR) met the Phase 1 permit test (R-25) is not yet confirmed in writing; nor is the "designed and approved" half of §12(a).
  • The SDR findings say the 602 house will be razed. Every option keeps it, so fold that into the minor modification the planner has said staff can approve.

Floodplain

  • Effective FIRM dated 2 Jun 2011 (PMC Ch. 18.55). Each structure needs a floodplain permit, no fill, flow-through vents and a site no-rise report (Cond. 3). The DA fixes floodplain rules by permit-application date.
  • The draft FEMA map lowering the BFE by 2 ft is not effective; FEMA's standard position is that a lower preliminary BFE can't be used until it is. Philomath's choice of FEMA no-net-loss compliance path is still to confirm.

Wetland

  • Wetland 1 (0.21 ac) and the pond (0.03 ac) are under the state Removal-Fill Law (50 CY threshold); the DSL concurrence runs to Sep 2030. Condition 2 puts wetland permits ahead of any building permit.

Fees and the code update

  • 2026 SDCs (Res. 25-29, 26-05): water $10,013, sewer $7,742, transportation $6,102, parks $6,003 per home, plus storm at $0.989 per SF of new impervious. About $29,900 a home before storm; rates index each 1 Jan.
  • The City's housing code audit (Aug 2026) proposes a new cottage cluster section, clear-and-objective review, and dropping ADU owner-occupancy and parking; adoption is expected around summer 2027. The DA's product cap holds either way.
