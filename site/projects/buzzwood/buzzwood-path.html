What the map shows

  • The record lives in Planning. Nearly every MKA Planning item is marked, plus the counsel items that are negotiation and interpretation rather than filing and hearings.
  • It re-opens Acquisition. Buzzwood is entitled, but the owned-land feasibility test (Phase 01’s gate) is still the open question. The record runs that test on post-entitlement facts.
  • It reaches into Preconstruction through cost and conditions. The owner’s estimate and the five-link condition tracking are there; design, contractor and baseline are not.
  • It stops at construction start. Phases 04 and 05 are touched only where a condition already names its closure evidence or the operating model is a scenario.

Gaps the path exposes

  1. Baseline schedule (R-18) is missing. It blocks the Preconstruction gate and every condition timeline check. Owner · 03
  2. No land use counsel engaged. Seven interpretations wait on the planner and one (R-22) on counsel. Counsel · 01–02
  3. Capital is a finding, not a workstream: $2.05M peak equity, no lender term sheet (M-7). MKA · 01, 03
  4. The SDC Case is framed but not sent. Step 2 is worth $130K to $578K. MKA · 02
  5. Condo vs PUD structure is undecided, which holds up plat, HOA and lending choices. Counsel · 03
  6. Title, easements and CC&Rs are not in the record at all. Counsel · 01
