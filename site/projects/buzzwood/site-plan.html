Where the exhibits and the record differ

Record findings X-6, P-1 · planner, 20 Aug 2026
These sheets show the layout approved in January 2025. The record's current program has moved on:
  • The 602 house. The approval razes it; it was renovated instead and is kept as the guest house (X-6). The revised site plan the planner asked for should show it, and the existing northeast driveway.
  • Phase 1 size. Pro forma v0.4 builds 5 homes in Phase 1 (P-1). The planner said adding a dwelling to Phase 1 is a minor modification staff can approve.
  • The homes. The exhibits use unit types 1–3. The record prices 15 two-level 1,000 SF cottages plus the 602 house, under the 16-home cap.
