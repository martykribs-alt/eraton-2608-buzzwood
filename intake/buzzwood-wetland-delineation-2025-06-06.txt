The Study Area totals approximately 2.02 acres, the entirety of tax lots 1200 and 1500 on tax map 12612CD plus a section of public road right-of-way, at 602 S 15th Street, Philomath. [WDR §A]

No recent alterations were observed that would likely impact the character or extent of Study Area wetlands or other waters. [WDR §B]

The field investigation was conducted September 13, 2024. The Antecedent Precipitation Tool found conditions wetter than normal, but given the dry-season timing a two-factor delineation approach was applied: plots with hydric soils and hydrophytic vegetation but lacking hydrology indicators were considered wetland. [WDR §C, §D]

The entire Study Area is mapped as Coburg silty clay loam, rarely flooded, a non-hydric alluvial soil typical of rarely inundated floodplain edges. [WDR §D, Table 3]

A single wetland totaling 0.21 acres (8,953 square feet) was delineated in the Study Area: Wetland 1, palustrine emergent and scrub-shrub, HGM flats, covering about one quarter of the Study Area. [WDR §E, §H]

Wetland 1 appears to extend north onto tax lot 1100 and south onto tax lot 6001. [WDR §E]

Wetland hydrology appears to be driven primarily by direct precipitation and runoff from adjacent uplands, including roof drains and ditches, and is augmented by poorly drained clayey soils. [WDR §E]

A shallow excavated pond of 0.03 acres (1,089 square feet), classified PUBx, lies east of Wetland 1 on the same terrace as the home and outbuildings. [WDR §E]

Ditch A is an excavated roadside ditch, 3 feet wide and 214 feet long, along South 15th Street; it is not adjacent to wetlands within the Study Area. [WDR §E]

Ditch B, 2 feet wide and 70 feet long, was created by the previous landowner, appears to receive overflow from Ditch A, and ends in a buried pipe that conveys runoff into the pond area. [WDR §E]

The delineated wetland is not in the National Wetland Inventory. It corresponds approximately to the City's Local Wetland Inventory feature MR12 but extends beyond it, past the northern and southern boundaries of the Study Area. [WDR §F]

No wetland indicators were observed in the southwest corner of the property, where the Local Wetland Inventory shows a second feature. [WDR §F]

This report should be considered a Preliminary Jurisdictional Determination of wetlands and other waters and used at your own risk unless it has been reviewed and approved in writing by the appropriate agencies. [WDR §I]

Final verification of this wetland delineation is to be made by the appropriate federal, state and local jurisdictions. Before final design or any construction activity on the site, all appropriate regulatory agencies should be contacted to verify the findings and to obtain approvals and permits. [WDR Preface]
